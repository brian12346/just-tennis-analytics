Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

Seller Sage Pro (the "App") is an internal business dashboard operated by Just Tennis Inc. ("Just Tennis", "we", "us"), based in San Diego, California. The App brings together sales, inventory, purchasing and accounting information from the services we use to run our business. This policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and the choices available.

Who uses the App

The App is used by Just Tennis and the people it authorizes. It is not offered to the general public, and it is not directed to children under 13.

Information the App accesses

  • Account sign-in information: the email address used to sign in to the App, so that only authorized users can use it.
  • QuickBooks Online data (with permission granted through Intuit's authorization screen): company information, vendors, the chart of accounts, items, and bills, bill payments and purchase records that the App reads or creates.
  • Commerce data from the connected Shopify store and Amazon seller accounts: products, inventory, costs, orders and sales totals, and purchase orders.
  • Documents uploaded by users, such as vendor invoices and purchase order PDFs.

The App is designed for business records. It does not set out to collect consumer personal information. Order data from connected stores can include customer names or addresses; the App uses that data only to calculate sales and fulfillment figures.

How the information is used

  • To show sales, inventory, cost and purchasing reports for Just Tennis.
  • To create and update records in QuickBooks Online that the user asks for, such as entering a vendor bill for a received purchase order.
  • To keep product costs and inventory values in step across the connected services.
  • To secure the App and to fix problems.

We do not sell information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

Service providers

The App relies on service providers that process information on our behalf, only to operate the App: database hosting (Supabase), web hosting (Vercel), code hosting and scheduled jobs (GitHub), and an AI assistant provider used to run and maintain the dashboard. Information also moves between the App and the services it connects to (Intuit QuickBooks, Shopify and Amazon) under those services' own terms and privacy policies.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights, property or safety.

How information is protected

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access tokens and credentials for connected services are stored in an encrypted secret store and are never placed in source code. Access to the App is limited to signed-in, authorized users.

How long information is kept

Information is kept for as long as it is needed to run the business and to meet accounting, tax and legal record-keeping obligations. When information is no longer needed, it is deleted or anonymized.

Disconnecting QuickBooks

The QuickBooks connection can be ended at any time by disconnecting the App in QuickBooks Online (under Apps / Manage apps) or by contacting us. Once disconnected, the App can no longer access QuickBooks data. Records the App already created in QuickBooks stay in QuickBooks.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under California law, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and not to be treated differently for making such a request. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
