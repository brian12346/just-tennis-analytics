Privacy Policy

Just Tennis Finance (Seller Sage) · Just Tennis, Inc. · Effective November 4, 2026
Just Tennis Finance is an internal bookkeeping tool used only by Just Tennis, Inc. staff to view the company's own business bank and credit card accounts. We use the data only to run our own books and plan cash flow. We never sell it.

Who we are

Just Tennis, Inc. (“Just Tennis”, “we”) is a California corporation at 9925 Businesspark Ave, Suite C, San Diego, CA 92131. This policy covers the Just Tennis Finance application at finance.andersenlifestyle.com and the data it collects through Plaid.

What we collect

DataWhere it comes from
Your sign-in email addressYou, when your account is created
Names, types and last 4 digits of linked accountsYour bank, through Plaid
Balances, available credit and credit limitsYour bank, through Plaid
Card statement balances, minimum payments, due dates, last payments and interest ratesYour card issuer, through Plaid
Transactions: date, amount, payee, category, check numberYour bank, through Plaid

We do not collect full account or routing numbers, and we never see or store your bank sign-in passwords. Those are handled by Plaid.

How we use it

  • Recording business transactions in our accounting system (QuickBooks Online).
  • Tracking card balances and due dates so bills are paid on time.
  • Forecasting the company's cash flow.

We do not sell the data, use it for advertising, or use it to make credit or other decisions about anyone.

Consent and Plaid

Accounts are connected only by an authorized Just Tennis user, who chooses which accounts to link and agrees to share them in Plaid's connection screen before any data is shared. Plaid's handling of your data is described in the Plaid End User Privacy Policy. You can stop sharing at any time by asking us to unlink the account (see Contact), or through my.plaid.com.

Who we share it with

Only with the service providers that run the application for us, under their own security and confidentiality terms:

  • Plaid: connects to the banks.
  • Supabase: hosts the database where the data is stored.
  • Vercel: serves the application's web pages.
  • Intuit (QuickBooks Online): our accounting records, for transactions a user chooses to record.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it.

How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest. Only named Just Tennis staff with finance access can see it, after signing in. Credentials and Plaid access tokens are kept in an encrypted vault and are never shown in the application. Our Information Security and Access Control policies describe these protections in full.

Retention and deletion

  • We keep transaction and statement data as long as we need it for bookkeeping and tax records, up to 7 years.
  • When an account is unlinked, we revoke its Plaid connection and stop collecting its data right away.
  • On request, or if we stop using Plaid, we delete the Plaid data and connection tokens within 30 days, except records the law requires us to keep.
  • We review this retention policy at least once a year.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete data we hold about you or your accounts, or to stop collection. California residents have these rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act; we will not treat you differently for using them. We answer requests within 45 days.

Contact

Send privacy questions and requests to Just Tennis, Inc., Attn: Privacy, 9925 Businesspark Ave, Suite C, San Diego, CA 92131.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page with a new effective date.
