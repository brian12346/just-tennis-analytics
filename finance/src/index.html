PayPal

PayPal keeps its fees and sends the rest to the bank. Upload PayPal's activity download (Activity → Statements → Activity download · Balance affecting · CSV) and Seller Sage books the fees and checks the Paypal Clearing Account against PayPal's balance. Uploading the same dates again is fine.
